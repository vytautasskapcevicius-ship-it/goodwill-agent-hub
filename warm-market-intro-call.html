1Open warm — it's you

Hey [Client], it's [Agent]! Good to hear your voice — listen, I've got some news and you came to mind. You got a couple minutes?

They already know you. No corporate intro — natural and warm. If it's a bad time, set a real callback time.

2The news — what you do now

So — I just got licensed and joined Goodwill Financial here in Elmhurst. We're independent, so I shop the top-rated carriers and find what actually fits the person. What we really do is protect families two ways: the everyday stuff — auto, home, business — and the bigger picture — life insurance and retirement. Our whole thing is "protect today, prepare for tomorrow."

Keep it tight. You're framing, not pitching.

3Door-opener — the easy yes

Here's why I'm calling you specifically — as I'm getting started, I want to take care of the people I actually care about first. Easy one first: can I run a quick free review of your auto and home (or your business coverage), just to check you're not overpaying or missing a gap? No obligation — worst case, I save you a few bucks.

Lowest-friction ask. Most say yes — it's a favor to them, and it earns you the meeting.

4Bridge to Living Benefits

And honestly — the part I'm most excited about, and the reason I got into this, is the life side. Most people think life insurance only pays after you're gone. What I do is different: it's life insurance you don't have to die to use.

Pause. Let "you don't have to die to use" land — this is the hook.

5The three pillars (teaser)

Three things the right plan can do. One — it protects your family if something happens to you. Two — and most people have never heard this — it protects you while you're alive: if a doctor ever says you're critically, chronically, or terminally ill, you can use a big part of it while you're still here, for treatment, bills, lost income. Three — set up right, it can build money you pull out tax-free for retirement; for higher earners there's even a strategy called Kai-Zen. I won't do all that on the phone — it deserves a real sit-down. That's what I'd love to show you.

Teaser, not a lesson. The goal is curiosity + the meeting — don't explain the mechanics on the phone.

6Book the meeting

Let's grab 20–30 minutes — I'll do the free auto/home review and show you how the life and retirement side works, no pressure either way. Phone, video, or coffee — whatever's easy. I've got [day/time] or [day/time] — which is better for you?

Two-choice close. If they're married, ask the spouse to join — it's a both-of-you decision. Send a confirmation text before you hang up.

7If they hesitate

"I already have life insurance / coverage through work."
Great — most people I care about do. Two quick things: does it let you use it while you're alive if you get sick? And does it follow you if you ever leave that job? A lot of work coverage disappears the day you walk out. Worth 20 minutes to be sure there's no gap — and if you're set, I'll tell you straight.
"I'm too young / healthy for this."
Honestly that's the best time — it's cheapest and easiest to get when you're young and healthy. Locking it in now is the whole point; you can't buy it once you actually need it. That's exactly why I wanted to talk to you before life gets complicated.
"Just email me the info."
I would, but there's no honest one-size answer — it depends on your situation and what you want to protect. That's why a quick 20 minutes shows you your options, not a generic brochure. [Two times] better?
"You just started — are you even qualified?"
Fair question — yeah, I'm fully licensed, and I've got the whole Goodwill Financial team and carriers like National Life Group behind me, so you get the agency's experience, not just mine. And honestly, that's why I'm starting with people I care about — I'll take extra good care of you.
"Let me think about it."
Totally fair — and the meeting itself is the no-pressure part; that's where you think it through, with real numbers in front of you instead of in your head. Let's just put a time down; if it's not for you, no harm done. This week or early next?

8Confirm & close

Perfect — I've got us down for [day/time]. I'll text you a confirmation right now so you've got my number. Talk soon, [Client] — take care!
Say it right: Living Benefits / Accelerated Benefit Riders are optional — using them reduces the death benefit and cash value and may be taxable; not available in every state. Never call an IUL an "investment." Don't say "free" about the insurance or riders ("no additional premium" where true — the auto/home review IS free). No guarantees on approval, rates, or payouts — real numbers come at the meeting, subject to underwriting. Tax/legal questions → refer to a qualified professional.

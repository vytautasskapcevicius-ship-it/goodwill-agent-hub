The framework we teach

Most people only plan for one of these. Smart families plan for all five.

Every retirement and protection plan has to answer five questions. If your current setup doesn't, we should talk.

01

Taxes

Your 401(k) is a tax bill waiting for the IRS to set the rate. Tax-free retirement is a choice you make now.

02

Market Risk

2008. 2020. 2022. If a 30% drop hits the year you retire, you don't get those years back. Zero is your hero.

03

Inflation

At 4% inflation, a dollar today is worth 50 cents in 18 years. Saving isn't enough — growth has to outpace it.

04

Illness & Injury

Heart attack, stroke, cancer, ALS. Health insurance pays the hospital. It doesn't pay the mortgage.

05

Longevity

The new retirement is 30–35 years, not 20. Your income has to outlast you, not the other way around.

Why we exist

Built by immigrants. Built for families who refuse to leave the people they love unprotected.

I arrived in the US in 2003 after finishing university in Vilnius. I spent 2.5 years at Prudential Financial, then opened Goodwill Financial because I kept meeting families who had pieces of coverage — auto here, a small term policy there — and no one who could put it all together for them.

Twenty-plus years later, the mission hasn't changed. We educate first. We help families and business owners see where the real gaps are. And we make sure that when life happens — and it always does — the people we love still have options.

"You wouldn't drive a car without airbags. You shouldn't have a policy without Living Benefits."

— Vytas Skapcevicius, Founder & CEO

The big idea — Acres of Diamonds

Your goldmine is the book you already have. Before chasing cold leads, mine your existing clients — welcome them, review them, cross-sell them, ask for referrals, win back the lost.

For Goodwill that "mining" has a name: protect more people with living benefits. Every auto client with no life policy is a diamond. The job is a machine that surfaces them and drafts the touch — so the agency runs without you.

People · Process · Promotion

People first — Stromsoe's order is deliberate. World-class people run world-class processes, and great processes power relentless promotion. Every automation serves one of the three — free your people, harden a process, or amplify promotion.

The Foundation

Two layers under everything. They make no money — they protect the money the machine makes.

Resilience

Today all 11 automations run on one logged-in desktop — unplug it and the company stops, silently. Move them to Claude Routines (Anthropic cloud, laptop closed). Add a heartbeat so a missed run alerts you.

Governance & E&O

A written AI-use program (NAIC AIS), a human accountable for every client decision, and consent logging. AI-exclusion clauses went live Jan 1 2026 — undisclosed AI use can void your E&O. Documentation is the asset.

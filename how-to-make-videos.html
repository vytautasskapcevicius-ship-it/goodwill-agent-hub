How to Make the Videos

Start to finish: how to shoot, caption, edit, and post your short-form videos — on a phone. Pair this with your script hub (the scripts + teleprompter). This is the how; the hub is the what.

📌 Golden rule: your script is in your language (Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, English…). The on-screen captions are in English — that's what wins mute viewers and reach. Film in your language, caption in English.

TL;DR — the 10-second version

  1. Phone on a tripod, vertical, eye level. Face a window. Clip on a mic.
  2. Hook in the first 3 seconds with English text already on screen. No "hi, welcome."
  3. Read from the teleprompter, but talk like you're at a kitchen table — to one person.
  4. One idea per video. 45–60s. Plus a 25–30s tight cut for TikTok.
  5. Auto-caption in CapCut (free), keep text in the safe zone, add the end card (logo + disclaimer).
  6. Post native (no TikTok watermark on Instagram), Mon/Wed/Fri. Pin your link in the first comment. Reply to every DM.
  7. Batch it: 10–13 videos in one 60–90 min session, once a month.

The two that matter most: light (face a window — never have it behind you) and a mic (bad audio kills more videos than bad video). 85%+ of top creators shoot on a phone — you don't need a real camera.

2 · Set the shot

  • Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 1080p. (4K just gets downscaled.)
  • Eye level — not up your nose, not down at you.
  • Eyes about 1/3 from the top, a little room above your head, face fills a good part of the frame.
  • Look at the LENS, not your own face on the screen.
  • Simple, on-brand background. Do a 5-second test: lit face, clear audio, nothing cut off.

3 · The safe zone (so your text isn't covered)

  • Keep all text, your face, and CTAs inside the center ~1080×1420 box. The top ~250px and bottom ~250px get covered by the app's buttons/caption.
  • Design for Instagram first — it has the tightest safe zone. Clean on IG = clean on TikTok & Shorts.
  • Instagram crops to a 4:5 window in the feed preview, so keep the key moment centered.

4 · The hook (first 3 seconds decide everything)

Up to half of viewers leave in 3 seconds, and ~half watch muted. So:

  • The English hook text is on screen from frame one (it's in your script's "0:00" cue).
  • Say the spoken hook immediately. No intro, no "hi guys, welcome back." First words = the hook.
  • Eye contact, energy up, the claim/number/question first.
  • Shoot the hook 2–3 times — it's the part worth re-doing.

5 · Reading & delivery

  • Use the teleprompter in your hub (▶). Put the reading device right next to the lens.
  • Talk to ONE person — like explaining to a friend at the kitchen table.
  • One idea per video. Cramming? Split it into two.
  • Numbers slow and clear. Natural beats robotic — a small fumble is fine, re-say the line and cut it later.

6 · Captions (mandatory — English, burned in)

  • CapCut (free) — auto-captions + styling; on export enable "Captions" to burn them in. Best free default.
  • Submagic (paid) — animated captions, auto-highlights the key word, emoji, B-roll suggestions. Fastest at volume.
  • Captions.ai — clean auto-captions, ~95% accuracy.
Because you speak another language, auto-caption will hear your language — so type the English captions using the "on-screen cues / caption" text already written in each script. It's done for you. Highlight the key word in each line; keep captions in the safe zone, big, 1–2 lines.

7 · Editing (keep it tight)

  • Cut dead air — first frame is already the hook. Trim "ums" and the run-up.
  • 45–60s main version + a 25–30s tight cut for TikTok (hook + core point + CTA).
  • Add the on-screen text cues at their timecodes (0:08 second-hook, teaching beats, CTA).
  • End card on every product video: logo + "Educational only. Not financial, tax, or legal advice. Products & availability vary by state and carrier."
  • Export clean — no watermark. Instagram penalizes TikTok-watermarked / recycled clips. Repost the idea, never the watermarked file.

8 · Compliance on camera (never skip)

  • Keep the disclaimer end card on every product video.
  • Never show an illustration or quote on screen. No policy-number screenshots.
  • Client stories: only with a signed release + carrier (NLG) pre-cleared template. Anonymize. Never film a real client without it.
  • Say "may / can / depending on the policy" — never "guaranteed," "tax-free," "can't lose money," "investment."
  • Not a licensed agent? Teach general concepts and send people to a licensed agent on the team for anything specific. You are the face + the invitation; the licensed agent handles details, quotes, and applications. Don't advise or recommend a product yourself.

9 · Posting

  • Native upload to each app — don't cross-post the watermarked file.
  • 3 a week (Mon/Wed/Fri). 3 strong beats 7 weak.
  • Caption: hook in the first line (~90 chars), short expansion, hashtags, compliance line.
  • Pin your link in your own first comment (booking / "DM me [keyword]").
  • Reply to every DM & keyword comment fast — that's where the lead lives.
  • Instagram: add carousels + Stories on the same topic. TikTok: stay in one lane.

10 · Batch filming (don't burn out)

  • Film 10–13 videos in one 60–90 min session, once a month — a month of content in an afternoon.
  • Change your shirt every 3–4 videos so it doesn't look like one sitting.
  • Work from a shot list (the week numbers in your hub). Film, then edit + schedule in a second session.
  • Keep a safety stock of 4–6 evergreen videos in reserve for sick weeks / vacations.

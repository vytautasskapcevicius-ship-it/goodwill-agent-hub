Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Goodwill Financial, Inc. and Goodwill Financial Group, Inc. (DBA Living Benefits Team) ("Goodwill Financial," "we," "us") respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you visit our website or work with us as a client.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, contact details, and the information you submit through our forms or quote tools so we can prepare quotes and service your policies.
  • Insurance information you choose to share — if you use our online quoting tools (including Canopy Connect), you may securely share details of your current policies so we can compare coverage. You control what you connect.
  • Usage information — basic analytics (such as pages visited) collected through tools like Google Analytics to improve the site.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, place and service insurance policies, and respond to your requests.
  • To contact you about your request, policies, or relevant updates.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.

How we share it

We share your information only as needed to serve you — for example, with insurance carriers and underwriters to obtain quotes and bind coverage, and with service providers who help us operate (such as our agency management and CRM systems). We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold, request corrections, or opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us. Standard message and data rates may apply to texts; reply STOP to opt out.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access to those who need it.

Quick answers

Before you call

The five things clients ask us most. If your answer isn't here, send us a request or call 630-785-2412.

I need proof of insurance right now — what's fastest?

Your carrier's app or website has your ID card instantly, 24/7 — that's the fastest route at a traffic stop or a dealership. If you can't get in, request cards here and we'll email them, usually within the hour during office hours.

I'm buying a car today. What does the dealer need?

Send us the year, make, model, and VIN, plus whether you're financing or leasing and the lienholder's name and address. Use the Policy Change form or call us — we can usually add the vehicle and get proof to the dealer the same day.

Why did my premium go up when nothing changed?

Usually it's not you. Carriers re-rate whole regions for repair costs, medical costs, and weather losses, so a clean policy can still see an increase at renewal. That's the moment being independent pays off: tell us and we'll re-shop your coverage across our carriers before you renew.

Does reporting a claim automatically raise my rate?

Not automatically. It depends on the type of loss, who was at fault, and your carrier's rules — and some claims (a windshield, for example) often don't affect your rate at all. Call us before you decide. We'll walk through what it likely means for your premium and whether it's worth filing.

Who do I pay — you or the insurance company?

It depends on the policy. Most personal auto and home bills go straight to the carrier through their app or website. If your invoice came from us, pay it here. Not sure which one you have? Ask us and we'll point you to the right place before anything lapses.

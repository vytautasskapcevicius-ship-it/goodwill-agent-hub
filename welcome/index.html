Day 8

Experience check → gated review ask

Subject
{{First Name}}, how are we doing so far?
Email — the sentiment check (sent to everyone)
Hi {{First Name}}, It's been about a week since you joined the Goodwill Financial family — I want to make sure your experience has been nothing short of excellent. Would you take 30 seconds to rate how we're doing so far? → {{1-click rating: great / okay / not great}} If anything hasn't been perfect, just reply to me directly and I'll make it right — that matters more to me than anything. Gratefully, Vytas Skapcevičius & the Goodwill Financial team
Auto follow-up — fires ONLY on a "great" rating (the review ask, at the peak)
Thank you, {{First Name}} — that means the world to us! If you'd be willing to share it, a quick Google review helps other families find protection they can trust: → {{Google Review Link}} Gratefully, the Goodwill Financial team
Review-ask timing (best practice): ask at a positive peak, not a flat date. The Day-8 email gauges sentiment; a "great" rating auto-fires the review ask immediately (while the feeling is fresh); a lukewarm/negative rating routes to the agent for service recovery — never push an unhappy client to a public review. Google nuance: don't suppress negative reviews ("review gating" violates Google policy) — ask everyone, just time the public-review invite to a high point; the reputation protection comes from catching problems early. Alternative peak: fire the review ask right after the First-Friday welcome call.

Day 45

Living-benefits educational seed (the North Star)

Subject
You protect your car and your home. What protects your family's income?
Body
Hi {{First Name}}, Quick thought — not a sales pitch. You insure your car and your home because if something happens to them, you don't want to start over. But the most valuable thing you own isn't your car or your home — it's your ability to earn an income and take care of the people who depend on you. Modern life insurance — what we call living benefits — does more than pay out if you pass away. The right policy can pay you, while you're alive, if you're diagnosed with a chronic, critical, or terminal illness. It's protection for the "what if," and for many families it can also build tax-advantaged savings for the future. It's worth a 15-minute conversation to see what it would look like for your family. No cost, no obligation — just information so you can decide. Want me to put together a quick illustration? → reply "yes" or call 630-785-2412. Gratefully, The Goodwill Financial team Living-benefit riders vary by product and state and are illustrated, not guaranteed. We'll show you the specifics for your situation.
Educate, don't pitch. This is the seed; the Day-60 call is the discovery. Compliance: illustrated-not-guaranteed caveat included.

"Protect Someone You Love" — the referral program

How it works

  • Engineered into Day-14 (flier) + every service high point (call script).
  • Dual reward: $25 gift card to the client + $25 charity donation (confirm amounts + state rules).
  • Capture: refer@goodwillfinancial.com + a refer page (QR on the flier) → Keap tag "Referral".
  • Each referral becomes a warm lead, routed to the right agent.

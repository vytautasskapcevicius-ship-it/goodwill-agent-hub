Call within 5 minutes of the lead — speed is your #1 advantage. No answer? Call + text + voicemail, then 6–8 touches over 10–14 days. Goal of this call: book the appointment, not sell.

1Open the call

Hi, is this [Client]? … Hey [Client], this is [Agent] with Goodwill Financial in Elmhurst. You went online and filled out a quick form about protecting your mortgage so your family keeps the home if anything happened to you — I'm the person who follows up on that. Did I catch you at an okay time?

Warm, smiling. This reminds them what they did — it kills "I don't remember." If it's a bad time, book a callback.

2Frame it + get permission

All this is: I'm an independent broker — I work with all the top-rated carriers, not just one, so I shop it and find what fits you best. Let me ask a few quick questions so I'm not throwing random numbers at you, then I'll show you a couple of options. Fair enough?

3Quick qualify

Conversational, not a form. Just enough to build real options.

4Discovery — make it real

Let me ask you something. If something happened to you tomorrow — is the mortgage paid off for [family], or are they left making that payment on their own?
How do you feel about that? … And why does it matter to you that [their words — the house stays, the kids don't have to move]?

Let them answer. Silence is fine. Write down their words — you'll use them to close.

5The Living Benefits difference

Here's what most people don't know. A regular mortgage policy only pays your family if you die. What I do is different — these plans also have living benefits: if a doctor ever says you're terminally, chronically, or critically ill — cancer, heart attack, stroke — you can use a big part of that money while you're still alive. So you're covered if you pass, and covered if you get sick and don't. That's what I want to walk you through.

This is your edge. Plain mortgage term can't say this. Say it slowly.

6Book the appointment

Here's what I'd suggest: I'll put together 2–3 options at different price points and walk you and [spouse] through them — about 20–30 minutes, by phone, video, or in the office. I've got [day/time] or [day/time] open — which is better for you both?

Always get the spouse on. Once they pick a time, lock it (Step 7) and text the confirmation before you hang up.

7Lock it in

Got it — [day/time]. Put it in your phone real quick: "[Agent], Goodwill Financial." It's important [spouse] is on too, since this protects both of you and you'll want to decide together. I'll text you a confirmation right now so you have my number. Talk [day]!

8If they hesitate

"I don't remember filling anything out."
Totally normal — it was a quick form on Facebook or Instagram about protecting your mortgage. No worries. While I've got you — is the mortgage something you've made sure is covered for your family, or not yet?
"Just email / text me the info."
I would, but there's no honest one-size price — it depends on age, health, and how you set it up. That's why I do a quick 20-minute walkthrough with your real numbers. Mornings or evenings better?
"How much is it?"
Fair question. It can run from about a coffee-a-day to more, depending on coverage and health — that's why I bring 2–3 options so you pick what fits. Quickest way is the 20-minute walkthrough. [day/time] or [day/time]?
"I already have insurance."
Smart — most people do. Quick question: does it actually pay off the mortgage, and does it stay if you ever leave your job? A lot of work coverage disappears the day you walk out. Worth 20 minutes to check for a gap.
"I need to talk to my spouse."
100% — you should decide this together, that's exactly why I want you both on for the 20 minutes. What works for the two of you?
"I'm not interested."
No problem — is it the timing, or you just don't think you need it? If something happened to you, who makes the mortgage payment? That's the only thing I'd want handled.

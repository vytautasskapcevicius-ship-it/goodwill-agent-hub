1Open the call

Hi [Client], this is [Agent] with Goodwill Financial. Did I catch you at an okay time — do you have two minutes?

Warm, relaxed, smiling. Wait for the yes before continuing.

2Why you're calling — Living Benefits

The reason I'm calling: right now we're educating all of our clients about living benefits. It's a protection plan that pays YOU cash when you need it most — cancer, heart attack, stroke, and many other serious illnesses. And it still pays your family in the event of death. Most people have no idea their coverage can work for them while they're alive.

Say it slowly. Let "pays YOU cash" land. This is the whole call.

3Ask permission

With your permission, I'd like to put together a personalized proposal for you — no cost and no obligation. Can I ask a few quick questions so it's accurate?

4Quick questions for the proposal

Keep it light and fast — only what you need to build a real proposal.

5Book the proposal review

I'll have your proposal ready by [day]. Let's set aside 20 minutes to walk through it together. What works better for you — [day/time] or [day/time]?

6If they hesitate

"I already have insurance."
That's great — most of what people have only pays after they pass. This adds a layer that pays YOU while you're still living. Worth 20 minutes to compare side by side?
"I can't afford anything right now."
Totally fair — that's exactly why we build it around your budget, not the other way around. The proposal is free, and you decide if it fits. Sound fair?
"Just email it to me."
I'll send it — but these plans are built around you, so 20 minutes on a call makes it genuinely useful instead of a generic quote. Quick call this week?
"I need to talk to my spouse."
Absolutely — let's get them on the call too, it's their protection as well. When are you both free for 20 minutes?
"Not interested."
No problem at all. Can I at least send you a one-page overview so you have it if anything ever changes? What's the best email?

7Confirm & close

Perfect. I've got us down for [day/time]. I'll text you a confirmation right now. Talk soon, [Client] — take care!

First Meeting Script

Build trust, uncover the need, set up the plan.

1Welcome & rapport

[Client], thank you for making the time today. Before anything else, I just want to get to know you a little. How long have you and your family been here in the area?

Spend 2–3 minutes here. People buy from people they trust. Find common ground first.

2Set the agenda

Here's how I'd like to use our time: first I'll get to know you and your family, then I'll show you how living benefits actually work, and if it's a fit, we'll look at a couple of options together. Does that sound fair?

Always get the small "yes" here — it frames the whole meeting.

3Discovery — listen and fill it in together

Let's start with you. Tell me about your family — who's counting on you, what you do for work, and what you'd most want protected if life ever threw a curveball.

Open the fact-finder and complete it together. Listen more than you talk. Jot the highlights below.

4Teach Living Benefits

Most people think life insurance only pays when you die. The plans we use are different — it's life insurance you don't have to die to use. Money really has three jobs: protect your family if you pass too soon, give you tax-free income when you retire, and help you financially if you get seriously sick along the way — cancer, heart attack, stroke. One plan can do all three.

Draw it if you can: three circles — Die too soon · Live too long · Get sick. Living benefits + IUL cover all three.

5Make it personal

Earlier you told me what matters most to you. Here's exactly how this plan addresses that, in plain terms…

Tie the concept back to the goals they marked on the fact-finder. Use their own words, not jargon.

6Trial close & transition

Based on everything you've shared — does protecting your family while building tax-free money for your own future make sense to you?

If yes → "Great — let me build you a proposal tailored to your exact numbers." If unsure → ask gently what's holding them back.

7Next steps

Here's what happens next: I'll build your personalized proposal, and we'll meet again to walk through it together. Please bring a recent pay stub and any current policy. What day works to review it?

Set the next appointment now, on the calendar. And ask for one referral before you leave.

8If they hesitate

"I need to think about it."
Of course — what specifically would you want to think through? Let's look at it together right now, so you're deciding with all the facts in front of you.
"I need to talk to my spouse."
Smart — this protects both of you. Let's set our next meeting with both of you there, so you decide together.
"I'm not sure I can afford it."
That's exactly why we start with your budget. We design the plan around what's comfortable for you — not the other way around.
"I want to shop around."
Good idea — and as you do, notice most agents only show the death benefit. Compare the living benefits too. That's where we're genuinely different.
